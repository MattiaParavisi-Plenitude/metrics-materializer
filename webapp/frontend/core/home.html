How It Works

One-Time Run (Ephemeral)

Submit a one-shot job that runs immediately and disappears after completion. Ideal for ad-hoc materializations, testing, or backfilling specific dates.

  • Runs immediately
  • No persistent job definition
  • Full dependency resolution
Scheduled (Persistent)

Deploy a persistent job with a CRON schedule. The job runs automatically at the configured interval and persists in the workspace.

  • CRON-based scheduling
  • Persistent job in Databricks
  • Same dependency logic
